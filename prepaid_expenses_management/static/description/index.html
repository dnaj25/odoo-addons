Prepaid Expenses & Rent Management
إدارة المصروفات المدفوعة مقدماً والإيجارات مع الخصم الآلي وتنبيه التجديد

A comprehensive, automated financial solution to manage prepaid expenses (Rent, Insurance, Software Subscriptions, Maintenance, and Permits) with scheduled accounting deductions, auto-posted journal entries, and last-month renewal alerts.

Rent & Lease Contracts / عقود الإيجار

Define multi-month or annual rental agreements for offices, retail stores, or warehouses with clear start/end dates and responsible partners.

Automated Periodic Deductions / الاستقطاع الآلي

Automatically generate and post monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual journal entries (Dr. Expense / Cr. Prepaid Asset) via Cron Job.

Last-Month Renewal Alert / تنبيه آخر شهر

Automatic reminder when entering the final month (or custom notice days). Sets scheduled activity and alert ribbons so you never miss a contract renewal.

One-Click Renewal / تجديد فوري بضغطة زر

Seamlessly renew expiring leases or subscriptions for a new period with pre-filled terms, updated start dates, and auto-generated schedules.

Live Balances & Analytics / الأرصدة ومراكز التكلفة

Instant tracking of Total Amount, Recognized Amount, and Remaining Balance with full Analytic Distribution (Cost Center) support.

Reports, Pivot & Kanban / لوحة تحكم وتقارير

Filter easily by "Expiring Soon", "Active", or "Expired". Analyze future expense commitments with interactive Graph and Pivot views.

How It Works / آلية العمل

1

Create Contract & Choose Category / إنشاء العقد واختيار التصنيف

Enter the prepaid amount, period (e.g. 1 Year), vendor/landlord, and select the category (Rent, Insurance, Software, Maintenance).

2

Auto-Generate Schedule / حساب جدول الأقساط آلياً

Click "Generate Schedule" to compute equal periodic installments with exact dates and rounding adjustments.

3

Automated Monthly Recognition / الخصم وقيد اليومية التلقائي

The daily cron job detects due installments and posts the accounting entries automatically: Debit Expense / Credit Prepaid Asset.

4

Final Month Alert & Quick Renewal / تنبيه آخر شهر والتجديد الفوري

When the final 30 days arrive, the contract turns into ⚠️ Expiring Soon, generates a renewal reminder activity, and enables one-click renewal.
