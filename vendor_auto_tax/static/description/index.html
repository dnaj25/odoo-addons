Vendor Auto Tax

Automatically apply vendor-specific purchase taxes to Purchase Orders

This module optimizes the procurement workflow by allowing you to configure a default purchase tax directly on each vendor's form. When adding items to a Purchase Order, Odoo will automatically apply the vendor's specific purchase tax, overriding product defaults to prevent manual accounting mistakes and save time.

Key Features

Vendor Default Purchase Tax Field

Adds a clean dropdown field on the partner form under the Purchase tab to define their tax rate.

Automatic PO Tax Resolution

Overrides product default purchase taxes automatically on PO lines upon item selection.
