Product Multi Barcode

One product · Many barcodes · Instant scan & search

Odoo 19.0 Ready

Key Features

  • Unlimited Extra Barcodes: Add as many additional barcodes as you need for a single product variant.
  • Dedicated Product Tab: View and manage all extra barcodes under the "Extra Barcodes" tab on both the product template and product variant forms.
  • Global Barcode Search: Automatically search and scan products by any of their extra barcodes across Sales, Purchases, Inventory, and Invoices.
  • Configurable Uniqueness Check: Toggle the "Unique Extra Barcodes" option in Inventory Settings to restrict or allow duplicate barcodes.
  • Multi-Company Support: Manage distinct extra barcodes per company in multi-company environments.

How it works

1. Open any product variant form and go to the Extra Barcodes tab.

2. Add extra barcodes with custom sequences.

3. Create a sales order, purchase order, or stock move and scan any extra barcode. The product will be resolved instantly!

4. Go to Inventory Settings to toggle barcode uniqueness validation rules.
